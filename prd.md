# PRD — Green Energy Solutions Business Portfolio

**Project:** Green Energy Solutions Corporate Website
**Version:** 1.0
**Platform:** Web
**Primary Goal:** Corporate branding + lead generation + digital business portfolio
**Recommended Stack:** Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + MongoDB + Resend

---

# 1. Product Overview

Green Energy Solutions requires a modern corporate website that transforms its existing offline brochure and business information into a professional digital presence.

The website will position the company as a provider of **solar energy solutions, solar EPC services, installation, maintenance and technical support** for commercial, industrial and institutional customers.

The website must function as:

* Corporate business portfolio
* Solar solutions showcase
* Project portfolio
* Lead-generation platform
* Consultation request platform
* Company information hub
* Long-term SEO asset
* Admin-managed content platform

# 3. Product Objectives

### Primary objectives

1. Establish a professional online presence.
2. Explain the company's solar solutions clearly.
3. Generate qualified business enquiries.
4. Showcase completed projects.
5. Communicate the company's capabilities and sectors served.
6. Provide direct phone, email and WhatsApp conversion paths.
7. Build search-engine visibility for relevant solar services.
8. Allow authorized staff to manage website content.

### Business KPI targets

The platform should be designed to measure:

* Consultation requests
* Contact enquiries
* Phone clicks
* WhatsApp clicks
* Email clicks
* Project page views
* Solution page views
* Lead conversion rate
* Traffic sources

No specific numerical targets should be assumed until the company establishes its marketing baseline.

# 32. Security

Admin functionality must include:

* Secure authentication
* Password hashing
* Session management
* Role-based access
* Input validation
* Rate limiting
* CSRF protection where applicable
* Secure HTTP headers
* CAPTCHA/bot protection for public forms
* Server-side validation

Never expose database credentials or email API keys to the client.

# 40. Final Product Vision

The final website should not feel like a **digital brochure**.

It should feel like a:

> **Professional solar engineering company with a strong B2B digital presence.**

The core user journey should be:

```text
Visitor
   ↓
Understand Green Energy Solutions
   ↓
Explore Solar Solution
   ↓
See Relevant Industry / Project
   ↓
Build Trust
   ↓
Request Consultation
   ↓
Lead enters Admin Dashboard
   ↓
Company contacts customer
```

**Primary business outcome:** turn the website into a measurable **lead-generation and corporate credibility platform**, while keeping the existing Green Energy Solutions brand identity intact.
